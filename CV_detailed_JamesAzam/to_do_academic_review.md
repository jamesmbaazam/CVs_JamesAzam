## Sources

Each item is tagged with the guidance that supports it:

- **[Cam]**: Cambridge University Careers Service, *CVs and Cover Letters for PhDs and Postdocs* (2016), https://www.careers.cam.ac.uk/files/phdpostdoccvbook.pdf. Page numbers are the booklet's own.
- **[Imp]**: Imperial College Careers Service, *How to Write an Academic CV*, https://www.imperial.ac.uk/media/imperial-college/administration-and-support-services/careers-service/public/resources/handouts/How-to-Write-an-Academic-CV.pdf
- **[Own]**: my own suggestion, not taken from either guide

Oxford's guidance (https://www.ox.ac.uk/careers/careers-guidance/job-search-and-applications/writing-applications/cvs) hasn't been read yet.

What both guides agree on:

- Academic CVs have no length limit, but shouldn't be padded
- Section order is flexible, and Education sits near the top
- Entries in each section are in reverse chronological order
- Publications are among the most important sections
